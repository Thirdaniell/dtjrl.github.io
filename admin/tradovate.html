Import from Tradovate

Export your trade history from Tradovate as a CSV and drop it here. FTJ will parse your fills, group them into trades, and let you import any one directly into the log form.

How to export from Tradovate
  1. 01  Go to trader.tradovate.com and log in
  2. 02  Click Activity in the left sidebar
  3. 03  Click Performance
  4. 04  Click the Export or download icon — save as CSV
  5. 05  Drag the file below or click to browse
Step 1 — Upload CSV
📂 Drag & drop your Performance.csv here or click to browse
